Description

Built for hands that don't quit. Heavy Duty All Natural Hand Soap cuts through the toughest grease, oil, and grime — on the job or off the clock — using natural ingredients that clean thoroughly without stripping your skin of what it needs to stay healthy.

Confirm any specific performance claims — must match MoCRA safety substantiation records

How to Use

Shake before use. Apply to wet hands. Work into a lather, focusing on grease and grime. Rinse thoroughly with water.

Ingredients

Aqua (Water), Potassium Cocoate, Potassium Castorate, Potassium Shea Butterate, Pumice, Glycerin, Simmondsia Chinensis (Jojoba) Seed Oil, Citrus Aurantium Dulcis (Orange) Peel Oil, Phenoxyethanol, Caprylyl Glycol, Sorbic Acid

Scent

Naturally scented with orange essential oil — a clean, citrus scent, not artificial fragrance.

Who It's For

Mechanics, welders, machinists, tradesmen, and weekend garage warriors — anyone who gets their hands dirty doing real work.
