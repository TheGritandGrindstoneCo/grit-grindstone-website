About Grit & GrindStone Co.

Hard work leaves a mark. For a lot of people, it shows up on their hands first — grease, oil, grime, and the dry, cracked skin that comes with scrubbing it all off, day after day. Grease doesn't care whose hands it's on. We believe hard work deserves real care in return. That belief is what led us to Grit & GrindStone.

We watched friends in the trades battle grease every day with harsh soaps that scrubbed the grease off — and stripped their skin right along with it. Dry, cracked, raw by the end of the week. We didn't think that should be the cost of getting clean.

So we set out to make something different: a soap tough on grease, not on hands.

Heavy Duty All Natural Hand Soap is built to cut through the toughest grease, oil, and grime you'll run into — on the job or off the clock — using natural ingredients that clean thoroughly without stripping your skin of what it needs to stay healthy. No harsh sulfates. No synthetic strippers. No chemical shortcuts. Just real cleaning power that respects the hands doing the work.

Because the workday already asks a lot of your hands. It shouldn't be your soap asking for more. Every wash with Grit & GrindStone is one more chance to repair what the workday takes away — to give your skin back a little of what a hard day's work wears down.

Whether you're washing up after getting elbow-deep in an engine, a shift on the job site, or a weekend spent under the hood, Grit & GrindStone is made for anyone who gets their hands dirty doing real work — and believes their skin deserves care, not compromise.

Grit & GrindStone Co. — tough on grease, not on hands.

Built on Principles. Made for Hands.
